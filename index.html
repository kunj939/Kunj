About

I studied physics to understand how the universe works. (Plot twist: the universe won.) So I walked out of my dual degree two years early, switched to statistics to understand how data works — and now I spend my days making machines think without ever calling the internet.

By day, I’m an Associate Consultant & Engineer in AI at Nomura Research Institute (NRI). When the firm wanted its own AI, I unboxed a brand-new E2E Networks GPU server and turned it into NRI’s first in-house AI platform — drivers to dashboards, models to agents, all running fully offline.

By night, I build products: Yaitro plans your next trip before you’ve finished packing, and TableBloom keeps restaurants running smoothly so the kitchen can focus on the food. Somewhere in between, I teach LLMs to read students’ emotions for research.
